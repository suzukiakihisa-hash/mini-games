Modified: child proportions, jaw/neck/nose/brows, shorter hairstyle and clothing fit;
51-bone/two-weight mobile skeleton; native eyelid/smile/cheek/jaw expressions baked
to two shapes; 1024px colour and 512px normal/roughness shared atlases; game materials.

for this project in Blender. Version 0.10 adapts the protagonist into a Japanese
primary-school boy: child silhouette, softer jaw/cheeks/nose, balanced eyes,
black hairstyle, brown irises and warm skin, with gentler posture and shorter steps.

as wholly original project models. The source and author do not endorse this game.

swept hair; native Snow lower legs and solid animated-eye topology; clipped shorts,
original socks, red/white shoe materials and projected brown irises/highlights.

no model or texture was purchased, downloaded or extracted from that product.
